Imagine a beautiful library holding the story of your entire life. Now imagine watching helplessly as the ink slowly fades from the pages, leaving nothing but blank paper. For millions trapped in the shadows of Alzheimer's, life is a living purgatory. You look into the eyes of the family you raised, and feel only the terrifying emptiness of a stranger's gaze. There is no worse fate, no deeper hell, than to lose the ones you love while you are still here to hold their hands. But what if we could build a digital tether back to reality? A lantern for the fading mind?
